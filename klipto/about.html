ABOUT KLIPTO SPORTS

Custom teamwear,
built around identity.

Klipto Sports provides fully customized team uniforms for sports teams, clubs, academies and organizations.

WHAT WE DO

Uniforms built around your team, not off a shelf.

Klipto Sports designs and produces fully customized uniforms for basketball, American football, soccer and ice hockey teams. Every uniform is developed around your team's colors, logo, names, numbers and specific requirements, rather than a fixed retail catalog.

Team-first approach

We work directly with coaches, team managers, clubs and academies to build uniforms that reflect their team's identity.

Design flexibility

Colors, logos, player names and numbers, sponsor branding and custom graphics can all be tailored to your team.

Sport-specific focus

We currently focus on four sports — basketball, American football, soccer and ice hockey — so every uniform gets the attention it needs.
