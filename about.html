ABOUT KLIPTO SPORTS

Your manufacturing partner for custom sportswear & apparel.

Based in Sialkot, Pakistan, Klipto Sports works with brands, clubs and businesses to discuss custom product development and manufacturing requirements.

WHO WE ARE

Product ideas shaped around your brand and requirements.

Our business is focused on custom B2B sportswear and apparel projects. We welcome inquiries from sportswear brands, clothing labels, football and basketball clubs, sports academies, retailers, importers, distributors and emerging businesses.

We aim to make the product development conversation clear: understand your brief, discuss design and specifications, evaluate a sample and confirm the details before bulk production.

Located in Sialkot, Pakistan, Klipto Sports is positioned to work with international customers seeking a manufacturing partner for custom products.
